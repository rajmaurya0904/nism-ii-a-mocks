# NISM Series II-A Mock Exams

Free, browser-based mock test practice tool for the **NISM Series II-A: Registrars to an Issue and Share Transfer Agents – Corporate Certification Examination**, built from the official August 2026 workbook.

## Features

- **50 mock tests** across three formats:
  - 10 Quick Tests — 30 questions, 36 minutes
  - 15 Half Mocks — 50 questions, 60 minutes
  - 25 Full Mocks — 100 questions, 120 minutes
- Chapter-wise weightage matched to the official exam pattern
- **Exam Mode** — timed, no feedback until submission (simulates the real test)
- **Practice Mode** — instant answer explanations after each question
- Progress saved locally in your browser — no login, no server, no tracking

## Tech

Single-page app — pure HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, no dependencies. Open `index.html` directly or host as static files.

## Disclaimer

This is an **unofficial study aid**, independently built. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or reviewed by NISM (National Institute of Securities Markets) or SEBI. Question content is for practice purposes only and does not guarantee exam results.
